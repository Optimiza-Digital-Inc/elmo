## Your First Brand

After signing in for the first time, Elmo shows the **Brand Onboarding** screen. This is where you tell Elmo about your brand.

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the brand onboarding screen */}

Your brand needs a name. Add your **Website** if the business has one, and use **Profile Links** for Google Business Profile, social media accounts, or other public profiles. You can leave both fields blank and click **Continue**.

## Set Up Tracking

With a website or profile link, choose **Analyze Brand** to research your market and suggest competitors and prompts. Choose **Set Up Manually** to enter them yourself. A competitor only needs a name. Add owned domains and profile links when available.

## Managing Brand Details Later

After initial setup, you can update your brand's details in **Settings → Brand**:

- **Name** — Your brand name as it should appear in AI responses
- **Website** — Your optional primary website URL
- **Additional Domains** — Other domains your brand owns
- **Profile Links** — Full links to your public profiles
- **Aliases** — Alternative names or spellings AI models might use

Elmo tracks mentions by business name and aliases. Website citation attribution uses owned domains.

## Multiple Brands

In local deployment mode, Elmo operates as a single-organization instance.
